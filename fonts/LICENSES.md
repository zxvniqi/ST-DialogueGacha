# Bundled fonts

The `.woff2` files in this folder are **subsets** of the fonts below (Korean KS X 1001 Hangul,
jamo, Japanese kana and JIS level-1 kanji, Latin and punctuation), made for this extension.
They are registered under the extension's own family names (`stdg-<id>`), not the original names.

All of them are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (https://openfontlicense.org),
which allows bundling, subsetting and redistribution. The fonts remain the work of their authors.
